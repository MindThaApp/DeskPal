## Description
Desk Pal is a friendly companion that lives in your Windows tray and helps you look after yourself while you work.

Long days at a desk add up: stiff shoulders, tired eyes, forgotten water. Desk Pal gives you gentle, well-timed nudges to stretch, drink water, rest your eyes and take a short walk. Bloop, a cheerful blue blob, shows you each stretch, and Sprout, a little potted plant, grows as you keep your daily goals.

Nudges that respect your day
• One smart schedule merges reminders so you are never flooded, and keeps them to a sensible number per hour.
• Desk Pal waits while you are in a call, while Focus Assist is on, or while a full-screen app is running, then picks a better moment.
• Choose which reminders you want and how often. Recommended timings are shown, and everything is adjustable.
• Eye breaks follow the popular 20-20-20 idea with a quiet on-screen cue instead of a noisy pop-up.

Bloop and Sprout
• Bloop demonstrates stretches in a live 3D view and reacts when you poke, pat or play with it.
• Sprout tracks your progress, so finishing a break feels like a small win rather than a chore.
• No guilt: missing a break never punishes you.

Made for your desktop
• Quick panel from the tray icon, a full app window to see today's progress, and a simple settings page.
• Follows your light or dark theme.
• Works with Automation Portal if you want breaks or focus time to trigger your own automations. This is off until you turn it on.
• Available in 20 languages, switchable any time in Settings.

Private by design
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your PC.
• Crash reports are only sent if you allow it.

Desk Pal offers general wellbeing tips and reminders. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
